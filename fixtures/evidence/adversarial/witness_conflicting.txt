On 5 September 2026, just before six in the evening, I was parked outside the pharmacy on Kingsway near the Harbour Road junction. The white Ford van was stopped at the red light. The silver Toyota came out of the car park behind the van, reversed quickly and hit the front of the van with its back end. The van had not moved. The Toyota driver then got out and started arguing with the van driver.
