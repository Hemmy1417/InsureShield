WITNESS STATEMENT
Name: Owen Hartley
Date of statement: 2026-09-07

I farm the land along Old Mill Road. At around ten past two on the morning of 7 September 2026 I was up with a lambing ewe and heard a heavy thud from the road, then a car stop. I walked down with a torch and found a silver Toyota on the verge with its front smashed in and its hazard lights on. The driver, a young woman in hospital scrubs, said a deer had jumped out in front of her. There were deer tracks through my hedgerow and the hedge was broken where she pointed. Red deer cross that stretch most nights in autumn.

Signed: Owen Hartley
