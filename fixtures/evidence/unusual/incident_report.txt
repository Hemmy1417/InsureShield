EASTFIELD POLICE - ROAD TRAFFIC COLLISION REPORT
Report reference: RTC-2026-119207
Date of collision: 2026-09-07
Time: approximately 02:10
Location: B4072 Old Mill Road, 1.2 km north of the Hartley Farm entrance, unlit rural road

Vehicle 1 (insured): Toyota Corolla, 2021, silver, registration EF21 KXT, VIN JTDBR32E720123456
Driver: Amara Okafor (registered keeper)

Account: The driver, a night-shift nurse returning home from Eastfield General Hospital, reports that a red deer ran out from the hedgerow on the left and was struck by the front of Vehicle 1. The animal ran off into the field. Damage to Vehicle 1: front bumper, bonnet and right headlamp. The driver was not injured and called the police from the roadside. No other vehicle was involved.

A breath test was administered at the scene and was negative. Deer fur was found on the front bumper by the attending officer, and the hedgerow was flattened at the point indicated by the driver.

Use of Vehicle 1: private (commuting).
