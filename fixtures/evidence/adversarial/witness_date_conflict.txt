On Tuesday 1 September 2026, in the morning, I was waiting to cross Kingsway at the Harbour Road junction. The lights were red for northbound traffic. A silver Toyota was stopped at the red light. A white Ford van came up behind it too fast, braked late and hit the back of the Toyota. Nobody seemed hurt. The two drivers swapped details.
