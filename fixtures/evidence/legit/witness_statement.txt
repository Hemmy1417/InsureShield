WITNESS STATEMENT
Name: Grace Liu
Date of statement: 2026-09-06

On 5 September 2026, at about twenty to six in the evening, I was waiting to cross Kingsway at the Harbour Road junction. The lights were red for northbound traffic. A silver Toyota was stopped at the red light. A white Ford van came up behind it too fast, braked late and hit the back of the Toyota. Nobody seemed hurt. The van driver apologised and the two drivers swapped details. I gave my phone number to the Toyota driver.

Signed: Grace Liu
